ascii-magic-art

The animated ASCII on the cabinet screens, the flow-page headers and dividers and the 404 comes from ascii-magic-art (https://github.com/kailashsr38/ascii-magic-art). tools/sync_ascii.py copies its runtime, helper kit and only the listed pieces into ascii/, with the licence in ascii/LICENSE. The two photo-based pieces that keep the Unsplash License (ocean-sunset, starry-ridge) are never copied.

Upstream provenance, as far as the repository shows it: the files were copied once, in commit 45452d5 (2026-10-22), from a local checkout of the repository above. The checkout's commit and the package version were not recorded then, and nothing in the copied files states a version, so no version number is claimed here. From now on tools/sync_ascii.py writes the upstream commit to ascii/UPSTREAM.txt when the checkout is a git repository. The files are unmodified upstream paths: runtime/{ascii,player,glyphs,canvas}.js, lib/kit.js, and these 21 pieces under pieces/: alien-wave, block-drop, brick-breaker, circuit-lap-racer, circuit-trace, coin-run-platformer, dino-runner, frog-hop-crossing, gem-cascade, maze-chase, paddle-tennis, pipe-flyer, pulse-line, rainbow-warp-road, rock-blaster, snake-autoplay, stars-divider, synthwave-grid, tv-static, vector-space-duel and wave-divider. Fonts (self-hosted)

The pages serve their three font families from fonts/; no font provider is contacted. All three are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose complete text, with each copyright line, is kept beside the files (fonts/OFL-*.txt):

FamilyFilesVersionUpstream
Cinzel Decorative (Bold, Black)CinzelDecorative-Bold.woff2, CinzelDecorative-Black.woff21.002https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cinzeldecorative
Press Start 2PPressStart2P-Regular.woff23.000https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/pressstart2p
Space Grotesk (variable, 300 to 700)SpaceGrotesk-Variable.woff22.000https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/spacegrotesk

The woff2 files are the Google Fonts distribution TTFs, recompressed to woff2 with fontTools and otherwise unchanged (no glyphs removed, no renaming), so the Reserved Font Names are not misused. They were copied from another local project that vendors the same Google Fonts files with their OFL texts, not downloaded for this site.
