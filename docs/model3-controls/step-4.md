# Step 4 — one-off cabinets
1. Ski Champ has a pivoted two-plate platform, pole buttons and a three-button selection panel.
2. Default uses a thumbstick; body lean/rock is offered only as a separate pictured pre-launch choice.
3. Magical Truck Adventure has two independently pivoted pump levers and two pedal switches.
4. Each controller owns one player’s pump/pedal/start/credit; a thumbstick-pump alternative is available.
5. Exact outputs preserve SkiX/Y, SkiPollLeft/Right, SkiSelect1–3, MagicalLever1/2 and MagicalPedal1/2.
6. The catalog’s Magical Truck wheel label is not used: the emulator exposes pumps, not a wheel.
7. All three models were rendered on CPU; recognisable shapes, separate moving pieces, rubber/metal/plastic and bevels are present.
8. Eight light/dark SVG cards explain the choices; there is no conventional stick-and-buttons title in this catalog.
9. No physical cabinet precision, live controller/body interaction or headset acceptance is claimed.
