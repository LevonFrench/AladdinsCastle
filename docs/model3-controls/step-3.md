3. Each game has three per-game holds: one gun, two hands on one gun, or independent P1/P2 guns.

5. Exact outputs are AnalogGunX/Y and AnalogGunX2/Y2, AnalogTriggerLeft/Right and their 2 variants, Start1/2 and Coin1/2.
6. Gun X increases right and gun Y increases up, both raw0–255; no light-gun Offscreen input is assigned to analog-gun games.
7. Real grip/muzzle/pivot/secondary-button nodes are reused; absent pistol secondary buttons remain controller-anchored.
8. Twelve light/dark vector cards show the three holds for the two families; second-trigger in-game captions remain questions.
