- Keep per-cursor last valid in-panel move and wheel remainder. Button packet
  coordinates are not authoritative. First accepted down owns the held-button
  chord; other cursors cannot steer, release or scroll it. Suppressed held downs
  remain suppressed until their up, preventing surprise gestures.
- Freshness is current receiver/visible/focus epoch. No default elapsed-age
  expiry: stationary controllers may stop sending moves. Invalid/out-of-panel
  positions invalidate before clamping; focus/hide/receiver changes invalidate.

  milliseconds. Explicitly handle regressing clocks; never infer occurrence time
  from OpenVR `eventAgeSeconds`. No production freeze/slop/drag/filter changes.
- Cancellation must produce no activation on real Button, MouseArea, TapHandler
  or Flickable, including target destruction; a fresh subsequent gesture must
  recover. Choose cleanup based on these failing-first scene checks.
- Diagnostics distinguish raw/cached position, dispatch, owner and ignored or
  canceled reasons without text. Retain corner/key/Unicode/session coverage and
  prove native desktop mouse/wheel routing remains intact.
