## Contract decision — renderer v0.2

Stereo identified that backend-private depth cannot occlude runtime gun draws
after the backend returns. Added `ACVR_CAP_REQUIRES_SHARED_DEPTH` and
`ACVR_DEVICE_GL_COMPATIBILITY`, without changing layouts or ABI major. Missing
required depth is rejected before drawing and yields zero submitted layers.
Runtime-owned shared depth survives world drawing for guns; fixed-function GL
must reject GLES/core-only devices. The control-set format stays v0.1. See
`docs/renderer-interface.md`. This is a declared requirement, not a GPU test.
