## Numeric finding, now fixed

Finite perspective coefficients did not establish that their packed floats
represented the requested clip planes. With identity poses, scale 1 and
FOV [-.65,.8,-.55,.7], the earlier source returned OK for these inputs:

| API | Near | Far | Old near/far NDC | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GL | 1 | 1.0000001192092896 | -2 / 0 | -1 / 1 |
| GL | .10000000149011612 | .10000000894069672 | .900000022 / 2.899999955 | -1 / 1 |
| Vulkan | .10000000149011612 | .10000000894069672 | .450000011 / 1.449999978 | 0 / 1 |

Far is the next representable float above near. The lead's packed-coefficient
endpoint checks now reject all three unusable cases with BAD_ARGUMENT, leaving
the complete destination byte-for-byte unchanged. Adjacent angular endpoints
(.7f and its next float) also reject atomically.

Vulkan near 1 / adjacent far remains accepted and maps exactly to 0 / 1. That
matrix is representable; the guard correctly does not reject every thin span.
No remaining defect was reproduced in this bounded latest-source review.

## Latest-source verification

- Noncommuting anchor and eye yaw/pitch/roll compose correctly. Maximum matrix
  component residuals for view × composed world pose were approximately 1.88e-8,
  5.93e-8, 4.42e-7 and 7.47e-5 at scales .01, 1, 10 and 1000 (including scene-unit
  translation components); translations are scaled once.
- Yaw .8, pitch .4 and roll -.6 recenter the supplied head at target height 1.2 m.
  Position residuals are below 1.3e-7 m and retained pitch/roll matrix error below
  4e-8. Zero scale and vertical-forward recenter reject without destination edits.
- The lead's shared quaternion normalization correction is confirmed by an
  executed cross-path case: anchor q_y=q_w=sqrt(.5004), accepted norm squared
  approximately 1.0008, scale 10 and a 2 m ray. View and anchored ray return OK;
  the ray endpoint transforms into eye space as exactly (0,0,-20). The former
  rounded-quaternion mismatch is superseded and not reported as an open finding.
- The reviewed endpoint tests reconstruct GL [-1,1] and Vulkan [0,1] depth and
  Vulkan's flipped Y from the packed matrix, before writing either destination
  matrix. Other eye fields remain outside this helper's mutation scope.
