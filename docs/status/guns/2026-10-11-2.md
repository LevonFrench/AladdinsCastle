forward trigger grip, open rear handle and seven separate Remote button nodes.

two-axis moving stick and C/Z parts, with no muzzle or cable. All geometry is
script-authored; private reference frames remain outside Git, without tracing.

and the complete sequential factory-startup CPU build/sheet path.
