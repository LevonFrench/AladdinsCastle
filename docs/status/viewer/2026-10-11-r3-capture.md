# R3 Viewer: bounded input-free capture

The supervisor uses explicit executable/header hashes, a fresh isolated SBS User
and owned-process cleanup. Capture-only selects the game-caption disc-ID token,
allows up to 90 seconds for startup and a spatial candidate, then separately
bounds the changing pair. Near-uniform interior RGB and border/alpha noise are
rejected. Optional canonical DSU bindings and bounded settle are supported.
No OS input, Computer Use or VR initialization occurs.

Verified: eleven synthetic harness tests, eight native CPU/software-WARP CTests,
MSVC Release /W4 /WX build, privacy audit/regressions and whitespace checks pass.
Two earlier full profile runs each had an intermittent access-denied staging
rename. Its cause is unproven; no production retry workaround was added.

The first validation phase ended with white/black startup frames after a
pre-launch rename failure and its one diagnosed retry. The separately reviewed
readiness phase first returned black plus diagnostic text. Its single diagnosed
retry, with isolated OSD-off/active-title settings and a 20-second settle,
produced animated Wii safety-screen content duplicated across SBS halves.
The lead independently accepted actual animated safety content in SBS layout.
The tested public defaults now reproduce the isolated OSD-off/active-title and
20-second settle settings; future receipts record launch timestamps. Historical
absolute DSU timeline-start timestamps were not recorded and are not inferred.
No menu, mission, stereoscopic depth or
headset acceptance is established. Raw images, logs and recipes stay private.
The helper exited normally; Dolphin required termination, so no normal game
exit or game-completion claim follows.

The lead owns scripted DSU service and further runtime coordination. A system
prompt stops the item; unrelated prompts cannot be detected by this supervisor.
Hardware texture submission, physical input, calibration, chapter/co-op,
comfort and timing remain open. Existing WIP and failed evidence are preserved.
