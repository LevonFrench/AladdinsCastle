## Emulator integrations and optional native tools

The Hub package does not bundle the emulator programs. Setup source, patches and
build instructions remain subject to their own upstream and file-level terms:

- [Supermodel](setups/supermodel-vr/README.md): GPL-3.0-or-later upstream and patch.
- [PCSX2](setups/pcsx2-vr/README.md): GPL-3.0-or-later; pinned source and
  reference-only PenguinScreen2 provenance are in its `upstream-pin.json`.
  PenguinScreen2 profiles have separate terms and are not covered by this notice.
- [DolphinXR](setups/dolphin-vr/README.md) and
  [Flycast/hotd2-vr](setups/flycast-hotd2-vr/README.md): upstream
  GPL-2.0-or-later declarations; preserve the exact patched files' notices.
  Flycast's gun mesh has the separate CC BY terms above.
- [MAME Model 2](setups/mame-model2-vr/README.md) and
  [MAME guns](setups/mame-gun-vr/README.md): MAME 0.289 GPLv2 distribution with
  per-file exceptions, including BSD additions. Preserve upstream authors and
  exact file-level terms. The BSD notice template needs its attribution resolved
  before a binary release; do not infer a GPLv3 grant from this repository's root.
- [Retro host and cores](setups/retro-ps1-vr/README.md): the host and each
  separately supplied core have distinct licences. SwanStation and Beetle pins
  are recorded beside their patches. The optional Beetle Saturn depth patch's
  licence boundary needs review against the exact upstream files; a GPLv2 text
  alone does not establish an "or later" grant.
- [System 22 slice](setups/namco22-vr/README.md): preserve the
  [upstream notice](setups/namco22-vr/patches/UPSTREAM-LICENSE) and source pin.
  This synthetic slice is not a complete shipped Time Crisis native build.

Optional OpenXR tools use separately supplied Khronos headers/loaders. Preserve
the exact version's per-file notices and source/build provenance when packaging
them; [Khronos' licence overview](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenXR-SDK/blob/release-1.1.43/COPYING.adoc)
does not identify an arbitrary local loader binary. OS libraries and developer
tools are prerequisites, not bundled application components.

Installation recipes and emulator manifests may fetch additional upstream
releases. Their pinned URLs, hashes and licence metadata must accompany the
selected artifact's own licence files; this source index is not a blanket
redistribution grant for those downloads or their transitive dependencies.
