Step inside the game.

AC VR Hub installs and launches true 3D VR for arcade and console light gun and racing games. The game's own world drawn around you in stereo, a gun in your hand, a wheel you can grab.

New game profiles and emulator wrappers are added all the time.

Motion controls

Your hands are the cabinet.

Your motion controllers become whatever the cabinet had. Point one and it is a light gun. Reach out and it grabs a wheel. Squeeze the triggers and they are pedals.

  • Let go of the grip to take cover, squeeze to pop back out
  • Point off-screen to reload
  • Recoil and detents felt as haptics
  • A laser pointer for the Hub's menus inside the headset

Animated gun models

The right gun in your hand.

In the headset your controller turns into the gun the game was made for. 23 original models cover all 152 gun games in the catalog: cabinet pistols, console pistols, revolvers, rifles, shotguns and mounted machine guns.

  • The slide kicks back on every shot; triggers, hammers and pumps move
  • A gun in each hand for two-gun play
  • Aim follows the barrel, not a flat screen

Lookalikes, not copies: original shapes with no logos, released under CC0.

Sit-down racers

Ghost controls.

Racing cabinets appear around you as see-through ghost controls: wheel, shifter, handlebars, levers. Reach out and grab one to take over. Analog triggers become pedals, with haptic detents and force feedback.

Sega Model 3

Model 3, in true 3D.

supermodel-vr builds on the Supermodel emulator and draws the game's 3D world once per eye. Pick a view per game: Screen, Wide, or Inside, where you stand in the world itself.

For the late-90s gun and racing boards: The Lost World, Ocean Hunter, L.A. Machineguns, Scud Race, Daytona USA 2, Sega Rally 2. In development.

Nintendo Wii

Wii gun games, in VR.

Through DolphinXR, a VR build of the Dolphin emulator, the Hub sets up Wii light gun games for the headset. Your controller's aim reaches the game as a pointer, and pointing off the screen edge reloads.

The current main push, in tiers of five, starting with the two Resident Evil Chronicles games. In development.

PlayStation 2

PS2, in stereo.

A patched PCSX2 presents the game in stereo straight to the headset, with real depth, console-pistol aim, and a memory card per game.

Aimed at Time Crisis 2 and 3, Crisis Zone, Vampire Night and Virtua Cop Elite Edition. In development.

Prize counter

Everything is a file.

Games, VR setups, install recipes, control sets, guns and profiles are plain TOML you can read, copy, share and override. Adding a game is a folder, not a code change.

Built on the people already doing this work: namco22-decompile, Supermodel, Dolphin, PCSX2, MAME, Time Crisis VR, VC2VR, DemulShooter and many more. We install them, add the missing VR pieces and send fixes upstream.

The Hub is GPL-3.0. The shared VR runtime, libacvr, is MIT so any emulator or engine can link it.

The Hub

One click from owning a game to playing it.

Point the Hub at your games. It checks each file by hash, installs the right VR setup from its original sources, configures it and keeps it updated. Press Play, put on the headset, and you are in.

  • A library with marquees, flyers and filters
  • Installed games appear in your SteamVR library too
  • Theatre mode on a virtual screen where no 3D setup exists yet

You bring your own legally obtained games. AC VR Hub never ships, downloads or links to ROMs, BIOS files or disc images.

Scoreboard

Where it stands.

The Qt Hub builds and its catalog, scanning, install and flat-launch paths are covered by tests. The shared VR runtime and the Wii, Model 3 and PS2 setups are being built now. There is no finished Hub-to-headset game yet.

New game profiles and emulator wrappers land all the time, so the list of games keeps growing.
