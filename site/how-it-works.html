World 1

Insert coin: from your files to the headset

  1. 1-1

    Your files

    Point the Hub at your ROM, disc and PC game folders. Files are matched by hash or disc serial and read where they are. Nothing is downloaded.

    Clear
  2. 1-2

    Recipe

    Each game has an install.toml with one block per VR setup: fetch the pinned emulator release from upstream, extract, write config, add shortcuts.

    Clear
  3. 1-3

    Verify

    Receipts and hashes record what was installed, so updates and uninstall touch only what the Hub put there.

    Clear
  4. 1-4

    Launch

    Pre-flight checks, SteamVR started if it is not running, then the game's VR setup runs with its own settings.

    In progress
  5. 1-5

    In the headset

    The VR setup owns the OpenXR session while you play. Quit and you are back in the Hub.

    In progress
World 2

SteamVR panel: the Hub inside the headset

  1. 2-1

    Overlay mode

    The same Hub screens render off screen and are handed to SteamVR as a dashboard overlay, so the desk and the headset share one UI.

    Clear
  2. 2-2

    Always there

    A dashboard manifest registers the Hub tab. Whenever SteamVR runs, the Hub attaches itself as the panel, and it starts SteamVR when it is missing.

    Clear
  3. 2-3

    Point and click

    Open the SteamVR dashboard and pick the Hub tab. Your controller's laser becomes mouse input. Clicks, stick scrolling and keys are being fixed after the first headset session.

    In progress
  4. 2-4

    Play from the panel

    Play runs the same launch service as the desktop Hub. Steam library shortcuts launch the same game and VR setup.

    In progress
  5. 2-5

    Straight in

    The game opens in VR without taking the headset off. Quit and you are back in SteamVR, ready to pick the next one.

    In progress
World 3 · Bonus

VR pinball: the Visual Pinball X launcher

  1. 3-1

    Your VPX

    Bring your own Visual Pinball X, the BGFX build 10.8.1. The Hub never downloads it.

    Clear
  2. 3-2

    Your tables

    One tables folder is scanned in place. Only each table's metadata is read.

    Clear
  3. 3-3

    Desk or headset

    Two buttons. The Hub writes its own settings file per table to turn VR on or off. Your own VPX settings are never edited.

    Clear
  4. 3-4

    VPX's own OpenXR

    With SteamVR running, VPX draws the table in the headset itself, without an extra VR layer.

    In progress
  5. 3-5

    Controls

    A gamepad layout goes into a copy of VPX's settings. Hold Y for 3 seconds to close the table. Analog plunger and nudge are still to come.

    In progress
  6. 3-6

    Clean exit

    VPX's processes are kept together and asked to close, never force-killed, so scores get the chance to save.

    Not yet
World 4

OpenXR translation: each system into true 3D

Every route ends the same way: two eye images go to OpenXR, SteamVR puts them in the headset, and your controller's pose comes back as the game's own gun or pointer input. What changes is where the stereo hook sits. New game profiles and emulator wrappers are added all the time.
